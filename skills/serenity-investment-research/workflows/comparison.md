# Comparison Workflow

Use this workflow for peer comparisons, stock pools, or "which company is the better chokepoint" questions.

## Comparison Table

For each company:

| Company | Node | Evidence quality | Bottleneck strength | Financial quality | Valuation odds | Key catalyst | Main bear case | Score |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---|---:|

## Rules

- Use the same source standard and time cutoff for every company.
- Do not compare one company's audited annual report with another company's rumor or media quote.
- Penalize companies with unverified exposure even if the theme is attractive.
- Split "best business" from "best risk/reward".
- Explain why the winner wins and what would reverse the ranking.

## Minimum Evidence

Each compared company needs:

- Business exposure source.
- Latest financial source.
- Valuation source.
- At least one risk or bear-case source.

If evidence is missing, mark the row as `insufficient evidence` rather than filling gaps by inference.
