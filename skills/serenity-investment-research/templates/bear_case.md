# Bear Case Template

Use this before writing any final report.

| Thesis point | What would disprove it | Where to verify | Current evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Demand is real | Orders or deployments fail to materialize | Filings, customer announcements, shipment data |  | High |
| Supply is constrained | New suppliers or internalization remove scarcity | Customer BOM, supplier capacity, competitor wins |  | High |
| Market underestimates it | Valuation and coverage already price the upside | Market data, consensus, price move |  | Medium |
| Value capture is strong | Margins compress or customer captures value | Segment margin, ASP, contract terms |  | High |
| Catalyst is near | Catalyst slips or is non-economic | IR calendar, filings, customer timeline |  | Medium |

## Writing Rules

- A bear case is not a generic risk list.
- Every bear point must map to a thesis point.
- Include at least one "the market may already be right" argument.
- If the strongest bear case is valuation, say so directly.
