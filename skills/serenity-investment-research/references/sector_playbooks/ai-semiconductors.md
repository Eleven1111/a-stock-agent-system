# Sector Playbook: AI Semiconductors

Use this playbook for AI accelerators, HBM, advanced packaging, substrate, EDA/IP, testing, power, thermal, and upstream equipment/materials.

## Chokepoint Questions

1. Which part of the AI compute stack constrains shipment: wafer, packaging, HBM, substrate, optics, power, cooling, testing, or customer qualification?
2. Is the company a direct beneficiary or only adjacent?
3. Can the bottleneck be solved by capex within 12-24 months?
4. Who captures margin: chip designer, foundry, packaging vendor, equipment vendor, or material supplier?
5. What does the current valuation imply about 2-3 year capacity and margin?

## High-Priority Nodes

| Node | Evidence to demand |
|---|---|
| HBM | Capacity allocation, customer qualification, ASP/margin |
| CoWoS / advanced packaging | Tooling, substrate, interposer, bottleneck capacity |
| CPO / optics | Customer ramp, optical engine reliability, supply qualification |
| Power delivery | Data-center power density adoption, module attach |
| Liquid cooling | Customer deployments, rack-level adoption |
| Testing / inspection | Bottleneck proof, tool utilization |
| Specialty materials | Qualification, concentration, expansion time |

## Common False Positives

- Generic "AI chip" exposure with no datacenter customer.
- Capacity expansion without customer allocation.
- Revenue growth from legacy segments relabeled as AI.
- Static PE used for an early ramp without scenario analysis.
