# Source Grading and Citation Rules

Use this reference to make report claims auditable.

## Grades

| Grade | Source type | Examples | Allowed use |
|---|---|---|---|
| S | Primary source | Annual reports, quarterly reports, exchange disclosures, official filings, prospectuses, investor presentations, earnings calls, direct company IR records, regulator or standards documents | Core facts and core thesis evidence |
| A | High-quality secondary or mirrored primary | Reputable financial data providers, reputable media quoting primary docs, official transcript databases, direct post mirrors with original links | Strong support; verify primary when possible |
| B | Traceable third-party research | Industry reports, broker summaries, expert articles with citations, independent databases | Supporting context and triangulation |
| C | Weak but useful leads | Social posts, community summaries, screenshots, non-cited videos, unsourced sector maps | Lead generation only |
| D | Unusable for conclusions | Anonymous claims, broken links, unverifiable screenshots, contradictory rumors, promotional material without evidence | Do not use |

## Claim Types

Use one of:

| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `fact` | Directly stated by a high-quality source |
| `source-backed inference` | Logical conclusion from cited facts |
| `third-party summary` | Another party's interpretation |
| `researcher inference` | Analyst judgment from multiple weak or indirect signals |

## Evidence Standards

- A core thesis point needs at least one S/A source.
- A strong conclusion needs at least two independent S/A sources or one S source plus strong numeric support.
- A bear-case point can use B/C sources if clearly labeled, but material financial or legal risks should be verified with S/A sources.
- Do not cite market price without a date.
- Do not cite social-media claims as company orders, customer names, or revenue unless confirmed by S/A evidence.

## Citation Format

In reports, each source row should include:

| Source | Date | URL/File | Grade | Supports |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Use local file paths for downloaded filings and web URLs for online sources.
